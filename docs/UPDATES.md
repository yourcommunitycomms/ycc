# Updates

- 2026-10-28: Release 1, the first release. Everything it does is in FEATURES.md (user guide) and ALL-FEATURES.txt; the LAN storage server and the Ubuntu desktop commander are in NOMAD-SETUP.md. Later bug fixes are listed below.
- 2026-10-28: Commander tablet in Waydroid on Ubuntu/NOMAD (NOMAD-SETUP.md section 3) and the bridge, which links Waydroid or emulator devices to the WiFi phones with no change on the phones.
- 2026-10-28: The bridge (server/bridge, a startup service on the server) is also the tunnel relay for remote people: one forwarded port and DRYAD-row key each, set up on the admin page's new Bridge tab (on/off, status, people, a QR code each). Phone setup codes may now hold only the tunnel; scanning one keeps the phone's LAN login.
- 2026-10-28: Fixed: Settings > LAN storage location, Share, only marked it for Share config; it now sends it to everyone on the channel at once (they are asked 'Update LAN storage location?'), and the dialog has 'Send it to the group again'. Fixed: with the internet tunnel on, a phone sent only through the relay, so back on the group's WiFi it went silent; it now sends on the local network as well. The Bridge tab shows where each remote person connects from, and shows no QR code until the internet address is set.
- 2026-10-28: Share location: tapping it while sharing offers Stop, 2 hours, Until the app restarts or Always. Bridge tab: an External gateway section (LAN addresses, what to forward where, the internet address, Find my WAN address), Show key with Copy for each person, and the QR code links work again (they failed with an error).
- 2026-10-28: Map controls on the home map, commander view and full map: arrows, zoom in and out, and a centre dot (centres on me), all usable with a TV remote. On a TV the title bar always shows, with a TV mode button; in TV mode the remote's arrows move the map and + / − (or channel up / down) zoom.
- 2026-10-28: On a TV the title bar has a Menu button (TVs often show no overflow dots), listing the whole menu.
- 2026-10-28: Map controls are off by default: the full map's Zoom − / Zoom + buttons are now one 'Show map controls' switch, which shows or hides the arrows and zoom pad on every map.
- 2026-10-28: LAN storage: an address without /YCC (e.g. http://<server>:8647) now works; before, phones looked for updates in the wrong folder and saved their sync files beside YCC (those were moved into YCC). Bridge tab: each remote person can have their own address (e.g. the server's VPN address), used in their QR code instead of the internet address.
- 2026-10-28: End of setup (and once on phones already set up): 'Keep the app on screen (black screen)' offers black screen after 1 min or a black screen button, and explains that a closed or backgrounded app may miss live messages. Coming back to the app after more than 30 s away now asks the others at once for what was sent meanwhile (catch-up sync, not while radio silent); it shows unread in Chat.
- 2026-10-28: Setup (and once on phones already set up) asks 'Record all voice', ticked by default, noting it sets the same option in Settings.
- 2026-10-28: New option 'Request missed messages when away' (on by default; Settings, below Record all voice, and in the setup step when Record all voice is ticked) switches the catch-up on coming back to the app.
- 2026-10-28: The catch-up on coming back asks for everything since the newest message this phone has from anyone (at least 30 min, up to a day), not a fixed 30 min. The DIGEST flags byte now says how far back to answer from (bits 1-7, 12-minute steps); older phones answer from their own window.
- 2026-10-28: Missed live voice: coming back to the app also asks for voice recordings; phones with Record all voice announce which ones they will send (others then leave those out) and send them; they appear unread in Chat ('Missed voice from …, caught up'), and gaps are asked for once more. New chat kind 8 VOICE; recordings of one transmission share an id on every phone. Setup questions now show one at a time.
- 2026-10-28: Commander view: when someone comes back after more than 2 min offline and missed messages, a 10 s pop-up asks 'X has been offline for Y min and missed N messages on B. Send?' (Send / Don't ask again / Not now; Settings option below Request missed messages). Send puts those messages, map points and voice recordings on the channel again. Tested: with two recording phones holding the same clips, only one sent them.
- 2026-10-28: Catch-ups are set per channel: each channel on the Channels page has 'Request catch-ups' (on for A, B and C-H, off for Public), listen-only channels too (they ask and take answers for messages and reports, and never answer). It replaces the single Settings option; the setup step ticks it on all channels but Public.
- 2026-10-28: Settings, under the callsign: 'Run the setup wizard again' (asks first; settings not changed in the wizard stay).
- 2026-10-28: Full map: 'Resync POIs' (clears others' map points and expired ones, keeps yours, asks everyone for theirs) and a 'Points:' filter for which channel's map points show.
- 2026-10-28: Settings split into pages: the main page (callsign, Update from URL and Edit update URL, setup wizard, permissions, mode) links to Channel settings, Audio PTT and alerts, Home screen, Network and sync, Security, and Mission and data (also on the Data / files screen); About and How Modern DRYAD works are at the bottom. Update from URL asks for the address the first time (suggesting the LAN storage's Updates/YCC.apk), tests it and keeps it as 'updateUrl', a shared setting that network defaults can set. Audio page: Test left / Test right tones and Swap left and right (stereo listening).
- 2026-10-28: Update from URL (e.g. a GitHub release): when what it downloads is newer than the LAN storage's Updates/version.txt, the phone also uploads it there (YCC.apk and version.txt), so every phone with auto updates gets it from the LAN cache.
- 2026-10-28: Received burst voice waits for a clear channel (nobody talking live, this phone not on air or recording, no other burst playing), up to a set time (Audio page: play at once, or wait up to 15 s / 30 s / 1 min (default) / 2 min / 5 min), then plays in order; an optional call-waiting beep says one is waiting (on by default).
- 2026-10-28: Full map: 'Fake GPS: on / off' button. Radio silent tick on the Channels page and the Data / files screen (the same switch as Settings > Security). Map controls stay off by default.
- 2026-10-28: Obscure location (Settings > Mission and data, below Fake GPS): 0 to 10 km, 2 km by default. Others get a spot up to that far from your real position (the same offset each time, accuracy widened to the range); it is also the fake spot's distance from its centre. Share location with Fake GPS on asks: keep the fake spot, or share your real position, obscured or exact (0 km). Applies to ATAK positions too.
- 2026-10-28: Turning Fake GPS off (full map button or the Settings tick) while Obscure location is above 0 asks yes/no: Yes keeps obscuring your real position, No sets it to 0 km (exact).
- 2026-10-28: Setup wizard: after the callsign and quick set, an "Advanced or simple" note explains DRYAD sheets (Advanced: encryption, digital and physical messages, channel names, shared between trusted devices) versus Simple modes' fixed keys, before the mode choice.
- 2026-10-28: Units: metric (default) or imperial (ft, mi), in the wizard's callsign step and at the top of Settings; every distance shown or read aloud follows it.
- 2026-10-28: Share my camera: 10 and 15 pictures a second for Tiny pictures (larger sizes stay at 5 or fewer), and Send audio too (off by default). The audio plays only on the Tactical videos screen, for the people shown (or just the one filling the screen); older versions ignore it.
- 2026-10-28: Map menu > Emergency alerts (CAP): presets VicEmergency (all of Victoria: incidents, warnings, burn areas) and NOAA / National Weather Service (alerts in force at the map centre), or a custom CAP 1.2 / CAP-AU XML or GeoJSON address. Areas are shaded by severity with a marker to tap for the details; also on the TV screen. Off by default; slow mode checks every 10 minutes.
- 2026-10-29: POIs drawn as an icon can be turned (5° steps), sized 50% to 1000% and given a direction arrow, to line a unit up with its real formation and heading. Changing only these updates everyone's map without a spoken announcement. Older versions show the icon as normal.
- 2026-10-29: Full map: the buttons are now one row of icons (Me, All, POI, Sync, Points, Pad, Fake, Tiles; long press says what each does) so none run off the screen. Sync and Resync POIs are one Sync button that offers both. In landscape the buttons sit in a column on the right of the map.

- 2026-10-29: Chat: a Filter button (top right) to show or hide Messages, Voice, Pictures and files, Map points, Reports, and Status changes and notices; pinned announcements always show.
- 2026-10-29: Full map: Share (my location, same questions as home) and Ask (asks everyone on a private channel (A, B or another keyed channel, not Public) to share for 30 minutes). Others get a Share / Not now prompt, or share by themselves with Settings > Mission and data > Automatically accept location sharing (off by default). The map's icons are now two rows of five.
- 2026-10-29: Audio page: Clean up received bursts (off by default). Before a burst plays it is decoded and cleaned: rumble cut (high-pass), background hiss between words turned down, quiet speech turned up (up to 20 dB) towards a common level, with a soft limiter; the original stays in Chat.
- 2026-10-29: Chat filter: a Channel choice: the channel on air (default, with the ones listened to), All channels, A (Private), B or Public.
- 2026-10-29: Update from URL now defaults to the project's GitHub release (https://github.com/yourcommunitycomms/ycc/releases/latest/download/YCC.apk, always the newest); Edit update URL's middle button switches to the LAN storage. tools/publish-github-release.ps1 makes a release (run it yourself with your token).
- 2026-10-29: DRYAD Enigma: CW (Morse). A row above the pigpen buttons (now one row of four): the lock enters CW mode (stealth; PTT, VOX and voice in and out off, for a radio plugged into the phone's audio), Generate CW (encode) / CW decode (decode, from a file), Send CW (made files, newest first), Receive CW (listens, records each message until the clear air after it, decodes and fills the sheet ID, start row and cipher text), and ⋯ for CW settings (message format with %R%, %SheetID%, %StartRow%, %CipherText%, %Call% and prosigns like <AR>; speed, tone, repeats, clear air before and after, receive end gap) and CW files (play, decode, join, split at gaps, delete).
- 2026-10-29: CW settings: Put received CW in Chat ("CW Decoded: <plain text>" when it decodes, "CW received: <what was heard>" when not), and In CW mode, send private texts by CW (texts on a private channel or just to me are encoded on the sheet last used on the Enigma page, from its next unused row, and sent when the air is clear). CW mode is now kept across restarts and crashes.
- 2026-10-29: Full map menu: Bulk add points (one per line: Name, Icon, Lat, Lon, Notes; typed, pasted, scanned from QR codes or opened from a .csv / .txt file; unknown icons become a grey dot with the icon name in brackets) asks whether to send them to everyone on the channel or keep them on this phone, a layer name, expiry, and skips repeats (same name within 25 m). Export points (all, or one layer) as text to copy, a .csv to share or save, or QR codes (in parts if long). Map > Points can show one layer.
