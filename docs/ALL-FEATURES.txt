YOUR COMMUNITY COMMUNICATIONS - ALL THE FEATURES (release 2, 16 November 2026)
For MANET and offline messaging.

Plain text, one feature per line, grouped by area, written to be read straight through or read
aloud. "Untested" means it has not been tried with real hardware yet.


WHAT IT IS
- An Android app that turns phones and tablets into encrypted push-to-talk radios, with chat, maps,
  reports and field codes, over an OpenMANET mesh or any shared WiFi network. No internet, no
  accounts, no app store: the APK is sideloaded.
- Runs on Android 4.1 (2012) up to Android 14, phones, tablets and Android TV.
- An optional LAN storage server (a small Docker app, for example on Project NOMAD) keeps map tiles,
  app updates, missions, logs and the group's default settings, and makes the phone setup QR code.
- Open source under the Apache License 2.0, and it can be rebuilt by any AI coding assistant from
  the included prompt.

GETTING STARTED
- Setup wizard: quick set from a QR code first (or paste the code; a code with a callsign skips that question), callsign, the device role (in alternating colours: Phone radio, Commander, Public intercom, TV / display, Camera and security monitor, Network gateway), for a phone radio an Advanced or simple explainer then Advanced / Simple with a Profile list and an "I lead a team" tick, voice, power with an Anchored tick, location (automatic sharing, obscure range, Public / Cloud sharing), maps, the keys, then an All set summary with a setup QR for the next device. New installs open on the map home with the General profile (I have arrived, On my way, Running late, I need help now, Delayed, All OK, Call me, Let's meet here, Let's eat here, Car park, Share location until stop).
  keys, and the permissions screen.
- Phone setup QR code: one scan gives a new phone the LAN storage and its login, the tunnel server,
  the Public key, optionally channel A, OpenMANET and the home WiFi, then loads the group's default
  settings and DRYAD sheets. The phone checks the login first and shows what it will change.
- The setup QR code is shown on the server's admin page (Phone setup tab) or by any set-up phone
  (Settings, Phone setup QR code for new phones, with or without channel A).
- Channel QR codes (QR of A, QR of B) and Scan QR in Settings.
- Share these settings and DRYAD sheets with the channel: everything except callsign, PIN and
  phone-specific settings; each receiver is asked before anything changes.
- Permissions screen: checks every permission and battery setting and explains the phone maker's own
  power saving.

MODES
- Advanced: the full radio, DRYAD, maps and every tool.
- Simple modes for Scouts, Staff, 4WDing, Friends convoy and Caravan or camping: one private key plus
  Public, and eight quick buttons made for the activity (map points, messages, share location).
- 4WD and convoy extras: drop-out alert when someone is not heard for 2 minutes, and crash detection
  (untested) that asks "Are you OK?" and sends a rescue alert with your position after 30 seconds.
- Intercom mode for a wall-mounted phone or tablet: room buttons, talk to one room or everyone,
  hands-free, history of every call, gate camera, auto blackout, settings PIN, no Exit, and it comes
  back by itself.
- Tablet commander view in landscape: big map with contacts and channels beside it, broadcast to
  several teams at once, and every map point button of the group's profile.
- TV mode for a big screen: map, chat cards and cameras; Android TV opens it by itself.

TALKING
- Channels A and B plus extra channels C to H. The channel name is the passcode: the same name always
  gives the same encryption keys, so anyone with the name can join and nobody else can listen.
- A Public channel with its own key, and an optional OpenMANET channel (experimental, unencrypted).
- Full duplex, many to many: everyone on a channel hears everyone, mixed.
- Hold to talk, tap-on tap-off, VOX, a USB or keyboard PTT key, a headset button, or a Bluetooth PTT
  button that works with the screen off.
- Acknowledgements: "Heard by" after each transmission, and "Transmission failed" with Retry now or
  Retry later when nobody confirms.
- Call button: rings everyone, or one person, with a full-screen alert they acknowledge.
- Start tone before each transmission (optional).
- Burst voice: record while PTT is held and send it as one file, for weak links. On by default for new installs (the setup wizard explains it); the setup QR carries it; contacts show a small grey B beside people in burst mode.
- Mission mode (1 to 24 hours): this phone runs flat out, and every phone on the channel stays awake until it ends.
- Adaptive check-ins (5 s busy, 30 s, 1 min quiet) and Wi-Fi power saving when quiet; opt-in Deep sleep (experimental).
- Away status: the phone sleeps and checks in by itself ("auto check-in in about N min" for others). Mains power anchor keeps a copy for sleepers and warns the channel if unplugged for 10 minutes.
- Burst sleep windows (5 s in every 30 s when everyone is in burst mode) and radio silence check-in windows (secret schedule from the channel key, overdue notices).
- Rest GPS when still; a sleeping phone checks in when moved, plugged in or unplugged; media-key PTT buttons work with the screen off.
- Exit offers "Going offline as planned" or "Back in 1/4/8/12/24 h": no lost contact warning for you, and an overdue notice if you are late back.
- Dual watch: talk on one channel and listen to others, quiet mix or stereo, per-channel mute.
- Pin a listened channel for its own one-touch PTT beside the big PTT on the radio screen (blue = last voice heard there), with its own Text as voice button too.
- Radio silent: receive only, nothing at all is sent, and texts wait until the silence ends.
- Status: Available, Away (voice saved to chat), Stealth (nothing plays; automatic at volume zero
  or on silent).
- Record all voice (optional), and voice heard while away saved for playback.
- Bluetooth headset and wired headset support; audio page with devices, levels, a meter and an echo
  test.
- Possible gunshots alert (untested): three loud bangs in 10 seconds send an alert with your position.
- Internet radio streams (optional): public scanner feeds such as Broadcastify.

CHAT, REPORTS AND CONTACTS
- Chat per channel: messages, private messages, pictures, files, voice, map points and reports.
- Pinned announcements, resent to people who join later.
- Catch-up sync every 5 minutes so anyone briefly out of range gets what they missed.
- Reports: SALUTE, SPOTREP, SITREP, LACE, ACE, 9-line MEDEVAC, CASEVAC, MIST, POSREP, LOCSTAT, AAR and
  custom templates, one field per page, sent now, later, or as a map point.
- Video reports during a mission (recorded on the phone, uploaded later, never sent over the radio).
- Contacts grouped by team: status colour, battery, last seen, distance and bearing, a private note,
  Message and Call for each person and team, Remove.
- Acknowledgement mode for range tests.

MAPS AND LOCATION
- Home screen as a radio (big PTT) or as a map (map with a smaller PTT).
- Full map with everyone's last position, a compass list, your heading arrow and a corner compass.
- Baseplate compass shows the area's magnetic declination, with an optional declination offset arrow on the bezel.
- Offline map folders, online maps (OpenStreetMap, Google satellite and terrain, OpenTopoMap, Esri,
  custom), a see-through layer on top, and online for 5 minutes, an hour, a day, or always.
- LAN storage tiles: maps read from the server first and save every online tile to it, so the whole
  group builds one shared offline map cache.
- Map tiles (any offline sets and map types: street, satellite, topographic) and street routing data can be sent to another phone over the radio in one pack.
- Map points with icons by category, quick-point profiles (Friends, Security, Firefighting, Military,
  Emergency services, custom), shapes (rectangle, circle, oval, line, arrow, text box) and colours,
  photo points with their direction cone, move, edit, remove, announce, and sync with everyone.
- Share my location for 30 minutes or until stopped; it carries on after a restart.
- Send my location when I transmit (optional).
- Bearing and distance to the map cross, lat/lon and MGRS of the cross (lat/lon on one line, the whole grid reference on the next).
- Type a map point's position: GPS latitude / longitude (decimal or degrees minutes seconds) or a grid reference (full, square and figures, or 6, 8 or 10 figures in the map's square); it shows as "GPS: ..." or "mil: ...".
- Full-screen baseplate compass with bearings to points and people, true or magnetic north.
- Navigate to a point online (Google Maps or another map app) or offline on the app's own map, driving or walking, with turn prompts; missing street routing data is offered for download.
- Copy the + position (for pasting into Google Maps); Share asks clipboard or team.
- Personal map points: kept on this phone only, never shared.
- Offline routes: plan first, close roads at the +, go via points (convoys), share the route to the channel, a person or another app; automatic rerouting off by default.
- Route via points (small dots), waypoints (flags) and planned rest / meeting stops (cups) with names, times and notes, a route editor, and route notes shared with the route; waypoints and stops are announced while following.
- Map home by default (radio buttons home one tap away), a Shortcuts page with the current state (power, Mission mode, Anchored, battery, status, location sharing, radio silent, Fake GPS) and every shortcut, a short menu with a More screen grouped by job, Simple / Advanced channels pages with a shown / hidden eye on every channel, Settings in twelve pages (Me, Channels and listening, Voice and PTT, Alerts, Screen, Power and batteries, Location and privacy, Network, Security, Data and backups, Advanced and Region features, About and updates), a device role and team lead tick, and a setup QR code that can set everything (role, group type, callsign with an optional lock, Anchored, features).
- The app can open into the full map or TV mode, and the screen can be locked to portrait or landscape, or turned upside down.
- Calendar and notes: my calendar, a team calendar shared on the channel, both together in two colours, reminders with an optional buzz, and notes with pictures in the text.
- Protected notes and calendar entries: a padlock and a password (no recovery) keep chosen notes and entries encrypted on this phone only; they are hidden every time the screen opens until unlocked, and never shared or synced.
- Protected entries: 3 wrong passwords in a row erase them; Change protected password and Erase all protected entries; a protected calendar entry starts with no reminder, and "Add a Protected Meeting reminder" shows no details.
- Advanced and Region features (once Specialised menus): Channel array, Report to leaders, Tasks and notes, Calendar and notes, Battery power, Cast screen to TV, Relay admin, Cloud group, T.REX Arms Topo Map; Region: VicEmergency, Melbourne trains preset, station names, ARTC rail; More...: Project NOMAD, ATAK map menu items, OpenMANET, Internet audio. All off until ticked in Settings > Advanced and Region features, or turned on by a setup QR code; an Advanced setup list of short guided setups (Cloud Server, Join the Cloud Server, cloud group, tunnel, LAN storage server, cameras, batteries, ATAK and OpenMANET, channel array).
- ARTC rail network overlay (Australian interstate and freight track), kept for offline use once seen.
- Live trains on the map with their next stops (GTFS Realtime: Melbourne tick with a free key, or your own home town's feed - recommended).
- Download detailed map tiles along a route (about 300 m either side, zoom levels and map types ticked).
- Routes list in the map menu (saved and recent, with their markers); remembered closed roads are offered for later routes ("closed X ago: ignore it?").
- Geofences: an area shape alerts when a contact leaves it, on this phone or also to the channel.
- Offline routes warn when more than 500 m off the route (this phone, or also the channel).
- Fake GPS for training: a random spot near a chosen centre, marked "Fake" for everyone.
- Obscure location: others get a spot up to 0-10 km (2 km default) from your real position; turning Fake GPS off asks whether to keep obscuring.
- Units: metric (default) or imperial, chosen in the wizard or Settings.
- Share my camera: up to 15 pictures a second when Tiny; optional audio (off by default) heard only on the Video feed screen; telemetry overlay (position, heading with north arrow, tilt and roll, speed, time) burned into each picture; keeps sharing in the background with a red-dot notification; torch and blackout buttons.
- Reolink cameras on the LAN (no internet): find them with a network scan, sign in, pick the lens of dual-lens models; picture tiles on Video feed, TV and the Intercom; full screen with live video where the phone can play it, listen, and the floodlight; motion or a person, vehicle or animal brings that camera to the front (several share the screen; each stays up 60 s after its movement ends; the Intercom returns to Blackout afterwards); linked floodlights turn on together and off after a set time; optional motion alerts and camera offline / back online alerts to the channel (chat, read aloud, one small picture), also over the internet relay. Cameras with only Reolink's own port 9000 (such as the E1) work too, pictures and motion only. Talking to the camera is not supported.
- Battery power (a specialised menu, off by default, was Bluetti power): watches several batteries at once, read-only (it never switches outputs or changes settings; use the maker's app for that). Kinds: Bluetti power stations over Bluetooth, a Tesla Powerwall 2 through its gateway on the home network (no sign-in), and, untested so far, Anker SOLIX and EcoFlow power stations over Bluetooth. Battery percent, power in and out, time to full or time left; for a Powerwall also solar, home use, grid and an outage warning. The phone near the batteries can watch them in the background, share a short status on the channel (every phone sees it on its Battery power screen) and send alerts: low and critical battery, charging started or stopped, mains or grid lost or back, overload, and out of contact.
- Emergency alerts (CAP) on the map: VicEmergency and NOAA presets or any CAP / GeoJSON feed, areas shaded by severity.
- POI icons can be rotated, resized (50-1000%) and given a direction arrow; such edits sync quietly.
- Full map Share and Ask-to-share (private channels) buttons; optional automatic accept. Chat filter by kind of item and by channel (including Cloud group); every message shows its source (A, B, PUBLIC or CLOUD with a little cloud).
- Burst voice cleanup (optional): noise reduction and level evening before playing.
- CW (Morse) on DRYAD Enigma: message format, generate / send / receive / decode, CW mode (stealth), join, split, upload and download CW files.
- Bulk add / export map points (text, QR or file; Name, Icon, Lat, Lon, Notes), layers, repeat skipping.
- Tunnel per channel (arch icon on the Channels page) or for all channels; callsign shown on the Channels page.
- Map search for suburbs, towns and streets, offline (GeoNames + OpenStreetMap lists, shared via LAN storage) or online.
- Data Tile Management: tile stats and prefetch the current screen, or 1 to 200 km around the +, or the whole world at low zoom, at the zoom levels you tick, with a super slow mode for huge areas (phone, LAN cache, then internet; fills the LAN cache).
- Secondary map server for tiles, and a manual sync between the two servers through the phone.
- Exit says goodbye, so others see you go offline at once.
- After Exit, asks whether to reconnect the last channels and shows their remote state.
- Asks to turn remote off on the home network, and on when away from it.
- Power and sleep settings: Normal, Power saver, Ultra low (check-ins every max sleep time, 1 to 60 minutes, sound for missed messages) and Offline, with battery levels that ask to change.
- Offline: PTT records messages that send when someone is heard.
- Normal running for 3 minutes after PTT in the saving modes.
- Option for the black screen to come back on for calls and voice.
- Red light on a schedule.
- Missed call line in Chat under Voice, and a reminder every 5 minutes until acknowledged.
- Photo messages: several photos and a comment together.
- Map eye button and speed readout.
- Channel array: hundreds of teams with import, export, setup QR and printed cards.
- Team names before the callsign.
- Report to leaders: OK, Need help, SOS, location and message, sealed so only leaders can read them, with approved codes.
- Tasks and notes for a channel, team or person.
- Private contacts shared by QR.
- Text as voice: typed messages read aloud on every phone.
- Blackout with no light at all, and a legacy setting (lowest backlight) for older devices. Turning the phone while black does not turn the screen or bring it back; it stays as it was until the double tap.
- Map layer button: street, satellite or topographic, with ticks for ships, aircraft and emergency alerts.
- Map scale bar and a two-point ruler: distance, bearing and back bearing.
- Aircraft (ADS-B) from a MANET receiver or online feeds, and ships (AIS) from Open Waters, each
  shareable to the channel.
- ATAK: import and export map files, an ATAK map source for the LAN tiles, and an ATAK compatible
  switch (positions and All Chat Rooms; never private channels or voice).
- T.REX Arms Topo Map (a specialised menu, off by default; then map menu, Data import and layers): steps to bake an offline topo .mbtiles file
  on the T.REX Arms Maps site, import it into the app's own folder (size and bounds shown), remove it,
  and choose it under Layer as a map type, with the OpenStreetMap credit shown on the map. Imported
  files go to the cache server's own T.REX folder; other phones view the map from there as they pan,
  or download the file for offline use.

CODES AND SECURITY
- Modern DRYAD sheets: batches, validity times, names, colours, 5-letter IDs, random row order, huge
  AA to ZZ sheets, share by text, QR or radio, and a row can become a channel key.
- DRYAD Enigma (encode and decode with QR), challenge and response, and a how-it-works page.
- Pigpen cipher: write with a classic or DRYAD-row key, and decode by tapping symbols.
- Tools (Settings > Tools): make a QR code from any text (full screen, save or share the picture),
  scan any QR code and see its text (copy it, or open it if it is a web address), and open a web
  address in the browser (anything not starting with http:// or https:// gets a warning first).
- Automatic key rotation hourly or daily, and a read-only Public listen key.
- PIN for codes, channels and settings; two wrong PINs (or a triple-tap on the callsign) erase all
  of the app's data.
- Lock button, red light mode, blackout and a black screen timer.
- Clear mission data, and clear all data and exit.

NETWORKS
- UDP broadcast or multicast on port 47474, over WiFi, VPN, USB tethering or USB Ethernet.
- Extra peers and a small PC relay to link separate networks or emulators.
- Tunnel comms: a phone away from the group reaches it over the internet through the server's bridge
  or a relay phone, UDP
  with TCP fallback, end to end encrypted, one port and DRYAD row per remote person.
- Internet relay on a small cloud server: links several MANETs and people away from them. Each phone
  is a gateway on the MANET or a remote off it (or auto-detects by its WiFi address), only channels
  marked with the globe are shared, one gateway per MANET with automatic standby, relay mode Direct,
  Internet relay or Both, link down notices and a site status list. Every site and remote has its
  own key and all share one port.
- Cloud group: a community channel like Public with its own Cloud Server (relay); join by link, Scan QR or paste (a cloud group user on an
  internet relay, one key that many phones share), separate from your own relay settings. It shows as
  Cloud beside the other channels, and a share link or QR code carries only that group and its
  settings. Show receive-only code gives a second link and QR code whose holders can listen and read
  the group but not send on it (like Public's listen key).
- Share my location with public and cloud groups: off by default, with a big warning when turned on.
  While off, no position, check-in or map point goes to Public or a cloud group.
- Relay admin screen: with the relay's admin key, list the relay's sites and remotes (online, last
  seen), add one (the relay makes its key, the phone shows its one-scan setup QR code), rename,
  remove (cut off within seconds), and change the admin key.
- OpenMANET support (off by default): talk groups and an internet gateway switch for the node
  (untested on a real node).
- Project NOMAD screen: ask its AI and open its Command Center.

LAN STORAGE AND SYNC (ON THE PHONE)
- LAN storage location with a Test button.
- Auto updates from the server, logs, data files, DRYAD sheets and config backups, on demand, weekly,
  daily or every minute, and always on the home WiFi.
- Missions: files go under the mission's own folder, with a final sync before clearing.
- Config backups: load defaults (the app's or the network's), save and read my config.

THE LAN STORAGE SERVER
- A small Python file server in Docker with no add-ons, for Project NOMAD, any Linux box or a Pi.
- Map tiles and the APK can be read without a login; everything else needs it.
- Web admin page: Manage (folder sizes, delete old files, delete missions, delete tile sources),
  Files (browse, upload, rename, delete), Defaults (the group's settings and DRYAD sheets), Phone
  setup (the setup QR code), Sync (copy new files to other stores such as a Pi, on a schedule with a
  bandwidth limit, never deleting anything there), Bridge (links a Waydroid or emulator tablet on the
  server to the WiFi, and is the tunnel relay for remote people, each with a forwarded port, a DRYAD
  row key and a QR code), Updates (publish an APK), Login, Docs (licence and
  how to rebuild with the AI prompt) and Erase (back to an empty store for a clean snapshot).
- Destructive buttons always ask first; Erase needs ERASE typed.

ON A COMPUTER
- The commander (tablet) APK runs on a Linux or Windows desktop in an Android emulator, or in Waydroid
  on an Ubuntu desktop such as Project NOMAD, linked to the group by the bridge or the small PC relay.
  See NOMAD-SETUP.md.
