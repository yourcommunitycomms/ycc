YOUR COMMUNITY COMMUNICATIONS - ALL THE FEATURES (release 1, 28 October 2026)
For MANET and offline messaging.

Plain text, one feature per line, grouped by area, written to be read straight through or read
aloud. "Untested" means it has not been tried with real hardware yet.


WHAT IT IS
- An Android app that turns phones and tablets into encrypted push-to-talk radios, with chat, maps,
  reports and field codes, over an OpenMANET mesh or any shared WiFi network. No internet, no
  accounts, no app store: the APK is sideloaded.
- Runs on Android 4.1 (2012) up to Android 14, phones, tablets and Android TV.
- An optional LAN storage server (a small Docker app, for example on Project NOMAD) keeps map tiles,
  app updates, missions, logs and the group's default settings, and makes the phone setup QR code.
- Open source under the Apache License 2.0, and it can be rebuilt by any AI coding assistant from
  the included prompt.

GETTING STARTED
- Setup wizard: callsign, then "Quick set from a QR code" or "Skip quick set", then the mode, the
  keys, and the permissions screen.
- Phone setup QR code: one scan gives a new phone the LAN storage and its login, the tunnel server,
  the Public key, optionally channel A, OpenMANET and the home WiFi, then loads the group's default
  settings and DRYAD sheets. The phone checks the login first and shows what it will change.
- The setup QR code is shown on the server's admin page (Phone setup tab) or by any set-up phone
  (Settings, Phone setup QR code for new phones, with or without channel A).
- Channel QR codes (QR of A, QR of B) and Scan QR in Settings.
- Share these settings and DRYAD sheets with the channel: everything except callsign, PIN and
  phone-specific settings; each receiver is asked before anything changes.
- Permissions screen: checks every permission and battery setting and explains the phone maker's own
  power saving.

MODES
- Advanced: the full radio, DRYAD, maps and every tool.
- Simple modes for Scouts, Staff, 4WDing, Friends convoy and Caravan or camping: one private key plus
  Public, and eight quick buttons made for the activity (map points, messages, share location).
- 4WD and convoy extras: drop-out alert when someone is not heard for 2 minutes, and crash detection
  (untested) that asks "Are you OK?" and sends a rescue alert with your position after 30 seconds.
- Intercom mode for a wall-mounted phone or tablet: room buttons, talk to one room or everyone,
  hands-free, history of every call, gate camera, auto blackout, settings PIN, no Exit, and it comes
  back by itself.
- Tablet commander view in landscape: big map with contacts and channels beside it, broadcast to
  several teams at once, and every map point button of the group's profile.
- TV mode for a big screen: map, chat cards and cameras; Android TV opens it by itself.

TALKING
- Channels A and B plus extra channels C to H. The channel name is the passcode: the same name always
  gives the same encryption keys, so anyone with the name can join and nobody else can listen.
- A Public channel with its own key, and an optional OpenMANET channel (experimental, unencrypted).
- Full duplex, many to many: everyone on a channel hears everyone, mixed.
- Hold to talk, tap-on tap-off, VOX, a USB or keyboard PTT key, a headset button, or a Bluetooth PTT
  button that works with the screen off.
- Acknowledgements: "Heard by" after each transmission, and "Transmission failed" with Retry now or
  Retry later when nobody confirms.
- Call button: rings everyone, or one person, with a full-screen alert they acknowledge.
- Start tone before each transmission (optional).
- Burst voice: record while PTT is held and send it as one file, for weak links.
- Dual watch: talk on one channel and listen to others, quiet mix or stereo, per-channel mute.
- Radio silent: receive only, nothing at all is sent, and texts wait until the silence ends.
- Status: Available, Away (voice saved to chat), Stealth (nothing plays; automatic at volume zero
  or on silent).
- Record all voice (optional), and voice heard while away saved for playback.
- Bluetooth headset and wired headset support; audio page with devices, levels, a meter and an echo
  test.
- Possible gunshots alert (untested): three loud bangs in 10 seconds send an alert with your position.
- Internet radio streams (optional): public scanner feeds such as Broadcastify.

CHAT, REPORTS AND CONTACTS
- Chat per channel: messages, private messages, pictures, files, voice, map points and reports.
- Pinned announcements, resent to people who join later.
- Catch-up sync every 5 minutes so anyone briefly out of range gets what they missed.
- Reports: SALUTE, SPOTREP, SITREP, LACE, ACE, 9-line MEDEVAC, CASEVAC, MIST, POSREP, LOCSTAT, AAR and
  custom templates, one field per page, sent now, later, or as a map point.
- Video reports during a mission (recorded on the phone, uploaded later, never sent over the radio).
- Contacts grouped by team: status colour, battery, last seen, distance and bearing, a private note,
  Message and Call for each person and team, Remove.
- Acknowledgement mode for range tests.

MAPS AND LOCATION
- Home screen as a radio (big PTT) or as a map (map with a smaller PTT).
- Full map with everyone's last position, a compass list, your heading arrow and a corner compass.
- Offline map folders, online maps (OpenStreetMap, Google satellite and terrain, OpenTopoMap, Esri,
  custom), a see-through layer on top, and online for 5 minutes, an hour, a day, or always.
- LAN storage tiles: maps read from the server first and save every online tile to it, so the whole
  group builds one shared offline map cache.
- Map tiles can be sent to another phone over the radio.
- Map points with icons by category, quick-point profiles (Friends, Security, Firefighting, Military,
  Emergency services, custom), shapes (rectangle, circle, oval, line, arrow, text box) and colours,
  photo points with their direction cone, move, edit, remove, announce, and sync with everyone.
- Share my location for 30 minutes or until stopped; it carries on after a restart.
- Send my location when I transmit (optional).
- Bearing and distance to the map cross, lat/lon and MGRS of the cross.
- Full-screen baseplate compass with bearings to points and people, true or magnetic north.
- Navigate to a point in Google Maps or another map app.
- Fake GPS for training: a random spot near a chosen centre, marked "Fake" for everyone.
- Aircraft (ADS-B) from a MANET receiver or online feeds, and ships (AIS) from Open Waters, each
  shareable to the channel.
- ATAK: import and export map files, an ATAK map source for the LAN tiles, and an ATAK compatible
  switch (positions and All Chat Rooms; never private channels or voice).

CODES AND SECURITY
- Modern DRYAD sheets: batches, validity times, names, colours, 5-letter IDs, random row order, huge
  AA to ZZ sheets, share by text, QR or radio, and a row can become a channel key.
- DRYAD Enigma (encode and decode with QR), challenge and response, and a how-it-works page.
- Pigpen cipher: write with a classic or DRYAD-row key, and decode by tapping symbols.
- Automatic key rotation hourly or daily, and a read-only Public listen key.
- PIN for codes, channels and settings; two wrong PINs (or a triple-tap on the callsign) erase all
  of the app's data.
- Lock button, red light mode, blackout and a black screen timer.
- Clear mission data, and clear all data and exit.

NETWORKS
- UDP broadcast or multicast on port 47474, over WiFi, VPN, USB tethering or USB Ethernet.
- Extra peers and a small PC relay to link separate networks or emulators.
- Tunnel comms: a phone away from the group reaches it over the internet through the server's bridge
  or a relay phone, UDP
  with TCP fallback, end to end encrypted, one port and DRYAD row per remote person.
- OpenMANET support (off by default): talk groups and an internet gateway switch for the node
  (untested on a real node).
- Project NOMAD screen: ask its AI and open its Command Center.

LAN STORAGE AND SYNC (ON THE PHONE)
- LAN storage location with a Test button.
- Auto updates from the server, logs, data files, DRYAD sheets and config backups, on demand, weekly,
  daily or every minute, and always on the home WiFi.
- Missions: files go under the mission's own folder, with a final sync before clearing.
- Config backups: load defaults (the app's or the network's), save and read my config.

THE LAN STORAGE SERVER
- A small Python file server in Docker with no add-ons, for Project NOMAD, any Linux box or a Pi.
- Map tiles and the APK can be read without a login; everything else needs it.
- Web admin page: Manage (folder sizes, delete old files, delete missions, delete tile sources),
  Files (browse, upload, rename, delete), Defaults (the group's settings and DRYAD sheets), Phone
  setup (the setup QR code), Sync (copy new files to other stores such as a Pi, on a schedule with a
  bandwidth limit, never deleting anything there), Bridge (links a Waydroid or emulator tablet on the
  server to the WiFi, and is the tunnel relay for remote people, each with a forwarded port, a DRYAD
  row key and a QR code), Updates (publish an APK), Login, Docs (licence and
  how to rebuild with the AI prompt) and Erase (back to an empty store for a clean snapshot).
- Destructive buttons always ask first; Erase needs ERASE typed.

ON A COMPUTER
- The commander (tablet) APK runs on a Linux or Windows desktop in an Android emulator, or in Waydroid
  on an Ubuntu desktop such as Project NOMAD, linked to the group by the bridge or the small PC relay.
  See NOMAD-SETUP.md.

NOT DONE YET

LATEST ADDITIONS (since release 1, 28 October 2026)
- Phone setup QR codes can carry only a tunnel (remote people); scanning keeps the phone's LAN login.
- Catch-ups per channel ("Request catch-ups" on the Channels page, Public off), on coming back to the
  app too, reaching back to the last message received (up to a day); listen-only channels can ask.
- Missed live voice comes back from phones that record all voice: one phone announces and sends, the
  others leave those clips out; they arrive unread in Chat.
- Commander view: "X has been offline for Y min and missed N messages. Send?" for 10 seconds.
- Setup asks about the black screen and Record all voice; Settings can run the wizard again.
- Settings split into pages (Channel, Audio and PTT, Home screen, Network and sync, Security, Mission
  and data); Update from URL (e.g. GitHub) that also refreshes the LAN storage's copy for everyone.
- Audio page: test left and right, swap left and right; received bursts wait for a clear channel,
  with an optional call-waiting beep.
- Maps: optional arrow and zoom controls, Resync map points, show points from one channel only, and
  a Fake GPS on/off button. Radio silent tick on the Channels page and Data screen.
- TV: a Menu and a TV mode button in the title bar.
- LAN storage server: Bridge tab (WiFi bridge and tunnel relay with per-person ports, keys, own
  addresses and QR codes), works with or without /YCC on the address.
